TRANSIT AUTHORITY SUPPORT

Passenger Knowledge Base & Contact

BackToyou operates across 18 terminal desks, providing verified handovers and passenger support.

Reporting Guidelines

Always specify line direction (e.g. Inbound vs Outbound) and approximate car number if known. Never list sensitive payment card PINs or master passwords in report descriptions.

Custody Storage Durations

Found property is stored in climate-controlled station holding vaults for 90 days. High-value electronics and wallets are audited weekly by transit security officers.

Collection Protocol

To release an item from station custody, passengers must present their digital Approved Claim QR Code or Claim Reference ID along with a government-issued photo ID.

Contact Central Transit Lost Property Desk

Our passenger support coordinators respond within 2-4 business hours.
